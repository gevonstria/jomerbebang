Casual Attire Guidelines

We kindly request our guests to dress in casual attire matching our coffee color palette (Cream/Ivory, Golden Beige, Coffee Brown, Rich Dark Brown, Chocolate Brown, and Deep Dark Brown).

For Gentlemen

Casual & Smart Casual

  • Cream, beige, or khaki linen/cotton button-down shirts (Foam or Crema tones)
  • Casual trousers, chinos, or slacks in roast, brown, or taupe
  • Optional: Casual blazer or jacket in earth tones
  • Comfortable leather shoes or loafers

For Ladies

Casual & Elegant Dresses

  • Casual midi or maxi dresses in crema, taupe, or earth tones
  • Chic separates or jumpsuits in palette colors
  • Comfortable footwear (wedges, flats, or block heels)
  • Minimalist accessories to complement the coffee theme
